==============================================================
 fm-decision-hold compatibility shim over fm-captain-hold
==============================================================

--- 1. A pre-collapse brief still registers its decision through the old surface.
$ fm-decision-hold.sh hold sample-route-review route --title ... --reason ... --repo sample
legacy identity -> sample-route-review-decision-route
captain-hold now owns it:
  task:
    id: sample-route-review-decision-route
    title: Choose north or south
    state: queued
    blocked: no
    blocked_by: none
    held: yes
    hold_reason: captain route choice pending
    hold_kind: captain
    hold_until: "-"
    kind: task
    repo: sample

--- 2. Follow-up work is durably blocked by the captain-held task.
  state: queued
  blocked: yes
  blocked_by: sample-route-review-decision-route

--- 3. Fork gate kept: decline WILL NOT release routed work.
$ fm-decision-hold.sh decline sample-route-review route --decision-file decline.txt
  fm-decision-hold: captain hold sample-route-review-decision-route still blocks routed work (ship-route); use resolve to record that work
  (exit 1 -- hold stays open, ship-route stays blocked)

--- 4. Fork resolve order kept: record the captain's body, then unblock, then close.
$ fm-decision-hold.sh resolve sample-route-review route --decision-file decide.txt --routed-to ship-route
  answered: sample-route-review-decision-route
  resolved: sample-route-review-decision-route -> ship-route

  captain-held task after resolve:
    task:
      id: sample-route-review-decision-route
      title: Choose north or south
      state: done
      blocked: no
      blocked_by: none
      held: no
      hold_reason: captain route choice pending
      hold_kind: captain
      hold_until: "-"
      kind: task
      repo: sample
      priority: "-"
      created: "-"
      closed: 2026-08-21
      deps: none
      links: none
      body: "Resolution recorded by fm-decision-hold.\nDecision digest: ea6d979e6e3a5117bcb8cf3ddd3b570d41e1a864206109f3c4acc60bd76b91c7\nRouted identities: ship-route\nResolution mode: routed\n\nCaptain decision:\nTake the north route.\n\nRouted work:- ship-route"

  routed work released:
    state: queued
    blocked: no
    blocked_by: none

--- 5. Idempotent replay of the same resolve is accepted (no drift).
$ fm-decision-hold.sh resolve sample-route-review route --decision-file decide.txt --routed-to ship-route   # replay
  answered: sample-route-review-decision-route
  resolved: sample-route-review-decision-route -> ship-route

--- 6. Fork gate kept: a spent identity cannot be reused for a new decision.
$ fm-decision-hold.sh hold sample-route-review route --title 'A different question' ...   # same key again
  fm-captain-hold: task sample-route-review-decision-route is already closed; a new captain call needs its own task
  (exit 1)

--- 7. A different key is still available, and drifted routes are refused.
  new hold -> sample-route-review-decision-route-two
$ fm-decision-hold.sh resolve sample-route-review route-two --decision-file decide.txt --routed-to ship-other   # not blocked by the hold
  fm-decision-hold: routed task ship-other is not durably blocked by sample-route-review-decision-route-two
  (exit 1)
